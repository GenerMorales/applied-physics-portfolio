# Applying Physics to Engineering and Technology

One of the reasons I enjoy studying applied physics is the connection between theoretical concepts and practical engineering applications.

## Electronics and Electrical Systems

Electricity and magnetism provide a foundation for understanding circuits, electrical equipment, and many modern technologies. I'm interested in continuing to develop my knowledge of these systems through academic and hands-on projects.

## Computational Physics

Programming and numerical methods allow us to explore physical problems that can be difficult to solve analytically. Computational tools can help simulate systems, analyze data, and test mathematical models.

## Aerospace Applications

Aerospace technology brings together several areas of physics, including mechanics, electromagnetism, and thermodynamics. I'm interested in learning how these principles contribute to the design, testing, and operation of aerospace systems.

## My Future Goals

I hope to develop a career where I can combine my physics education with practical engineering work, particularly in testing, electronics, or aerospace-related technology.
